AI Assistant, Rebuilt

A smarter model, real grounding in your own records, and the ability to actually look things up instead of guessing. Ask it about your own rank, your history, or another officer by name — not just @mention — and it answers from what's真actually on file, with a rank whitelist and a fact-check pass so it can't invent an answer that isn't real.

Self-Service Duty Fix

>duty fix lets you request a correction to one of your own recent shifts — wrong time, wrong type, or void it entirely — with a reason. A supervisor reviews and approves or denies it. You're never editing your own record directly, and you get a DM either way.

Auto-Promotions From List

Post a weekly promotion list, medal announcement, or Trooper of the Week callout in the promotions channel and the bot recognizes it, DMs you to confirm, and applies every rank role, nickname, medal role, and Trooper of the Week role for everyone listed — no manual role-clicking required.

Subdivisions, RA Overhaul, Live Leaderboard, AI Tools & Big UX Pass

Update #2 · 2026-07-02

The second major release: full subdivision integration so officers only clock in once, a completely rebuilt RA training system, a live auto-refreshing leaderboard, new AI tools for management, ERLC presence detection, and dozens of quality-of-life improvements across shifts, settings, and the bot's reliability.

  • SUBDIVISIONS — Starting a shift in a subdivision server (e.g. SEU) now automatically mirrors it to CHP as a linked shift. Officers see a confirmation line in the shift embed confirming the mirror succeeded, or a warning if they haven't joined the CHP server yet.
  • The CHP linked role is automatically assigned the moment a member receives their subdivision division role, or on their first shift — whichever comes first. An hourly sweep catches anyone who slipped through.
  • >subdivision roster — live on-duty list for the subdivision with callsign sorting, plus a 'Show Full Roster' button for all division-role holders.
  • >subdivision stats, >subdivision lb, >subdivision compare, >subdivision audit, >subdivision announce, >subdivision lock — full management toolkit for subdivisions.
  • Inactivity alerts fire to the subdivision channel when a member goes 7 days without a shift. Mid-week DMs remind subdivision members who are under 50% of their weekly quota.
  • RA SYSTEM — /ra request lets Probationary Officers post a training request to the channel with an optional availability window. Available FTOs are pinged and can claim it with one click.
  • /ra host supports two modes: Individual (one trainee, session auto-closes on join) and Bulk (multiple trainees join within a 10-minute window).
  • /ra results submits a Pass or Fail verdict for a completed session. A Pass automatically grants the Officer role, removes Probationary Officer, assigns the CHP role, and swaps the rank tag in the trainee's nickname.
  • /ra feedback lets trainees anonymously rate their FTO 1–5 on communication, patience, and clarity. FTOs can view their scores with /ra leaderboard.
  • RA fail limit tracking: when a trainee reaches the configured fail limit, a warning is automatically posted to the review channel.
  • LIVE LEADERBOARD — >duty livelb posts a pinned leaderboard message that auto-refreshes every 30 seconds without anyone needing to run a command. Includes a Browse button for an ephemeral paginated view with subdivision filtering.
  • SHIFT UX — The shift start embed now shows a welcome-back line: how long since your last shift and how many hours you've logged this week.
  • >duty active now has a 'Grouped by unit' sort mode that splits the active list into sections by shift type (CHP, SEU, etc.) instead of one mixed flat list. Category filtering (Whole Department / CHP Only / per-subdivision) also added.
  • Slow commands now show live step-by-step loading progress instead of a silent spinner — affected commands: /ask, >duty recap, >duty leaderboard, /ai detect/summarize/rewrite/tone, >app stats.
  • The bot's status rotates every 30 seconds: officers on duty -> shifts logged today -> officers on LOA — always shows live counts.
  • AI TOOLS — /ask now accepts image attachments (uniform checks, rank insignia, screenshots). It also uses community knowledge auto-indexed from your server channels every 30 minutes, and remembers past infractions when answering questions.
  • /ai detect — analyzes text for AI-generated content (0–100 confidence score with reasoning). /ai summarize — condenses long write-ups. /ai rewrite — polishes text professionally. /ai tone — flags conduct concerns and unprofessional language.
  • USER SETTINGS — /settings now includes accent color (9 color choices), mobile-friendly embeds toggle, per-user weekly digest day/hour, shift-end note prompt, and shift duration reminder DMs (while on shift, every N minutes).
  • Deputy-tier settings: default shift type (skips the picker), quota reminder day/hour, confirm before ending long shifts, auto-end shift after a threshold, and auto-toggle RA availability when going on/off duty.
  • FTO-tier settings: default max-trainee cap (skips the prompt when hosting bulk sessions) and DM alerts when your trainees receive a Pass/Fail verdict.
  • ERLC PRESENCE — The bot monitors when you join or leave the ERLC game. It prompts you to start a shift when you join, auto-opens a break if you've been away 5+ minutes, and auto-ends your shift if you've been away for 60+ minutes.
  • AUTOMATION — Stale shifts left open 16+ hours are automatically closed to prevent ghost entries. Service stripe badges are awarded at 10, 25, 50, and 100 total shifts.
  • #BOT-LOGS — A dedicated audit channel now receives a log entry for every significant bot action: every command used (prefix and slash), shift start/end/break, LOA accept/deny, RA events, infraction records, role syncs, and errors.
  • PRIVACY — Profile visibility can be set to Everyone, Staff only, or Command Team only. When management uses an override to view a locked profile, it is automatically logged to the server's audit channel.
  • RELIABILITY — Single-instance enforcement prevents two bots starting on restart. Slash commands no longer throw 'already responded' errors. MongoDB connectivity issues no longer crash the prefix handler. All custom emojis are now used throughout every embed and message.

CHP Management Launch - Recruitment, Applications, /ask & a Big QOL Pass

Update #1 · 2026-06-26

The first full release: automatic recruitment setup, a complete in-house application system with AI-assisted review, an AI assistant that knows CHP policy, a personal /settings command, an extra layer of tools for Deputies, and a long list of quality-of-life improvements across shifts and applications.

  • Automatic Setup resolves the Hicom, Officer, Probationary Officer, Denied, and Blacklisted roles, plus the applications-log, apply, hicom-announcements, and recruitment-report channels - creating anything that's missing.
  • Passing an RA session now also grants the Officer role and swaps PO to O in the trainee's nickname.
  • /config is reorganized into focused pages (Roles, Channels & Quotas, RA Training, Recruitment) with plain-language explanations instead of one long dropdown.
  • New application system: a DM-based pre-screening flow (privacy policy, background check, required reading, AI warning) followed by 23 application questions, with progress automatically saved if you time out mid-application.
  • Staff can Claim, Accept, or Decline applications, run an AI Detector and Summary, check applicant history and background, flag for priority review, leave notes, and view a reapplication diff - all from the application embed.
  • /ask - ask the bot anything about CHP (ranks, codes, policy, vehicles) and it answers using the official CHP knowledge base.
  • /profile - shows your weekly hours vs quota, shift streak, RA pass rate, and earned badges.
  • /dashboard - a management overview combining shift, RA, and recruitment activity in one place.
  • Accepted applicants now get a step-by-step welcome guide instead of a plain acceptance message.
  • Automatic weekly report card DMs, quota reminders for anyone falling behind, and a weekly recruitment report.
  • /settings - a personal settings command nobody else can change for you: private profile (hide your duty time from non-Hicom staff and leaderboards), timezone, mobile-friendly embeds, and opt-outs for inactivity nudges and the weekly report card.
  • Deputy-only settings (visible to Staff Role holders): a default shift type so you don't have to pick one every time, your own quota reminder day/time instead of the server default, an optional confirmation before ending unusually long shifts, auto-claiming applications you open, a default max-trainee cap for bulk RA sessions, auto-toggling your RA availability when you go on/off duty, which /profile widgets you see, and DM alerts for new applications or when a trainee of yours gets a verdict.
  • /myactions - a Deputy-only scoped view of your own recent application reviews and RA results, without needing full audit access.
  • /info - a dropdown reference for 10 codes, penal/vehicle codes, loadout, vehicle regulations, and FAQ.
  • /practice - a 5-question quiz pulled from codes and policy to test yourself anytime.
  • /lookup - quickly check anyone's rank, badge number, on-duty status, and join date.
  • A gentler daily check-in DM for anyone inactive a few days, separate from the harsher quota reminder, and smart enough to skip people who've been busy training recruits instead of logging a shift.
  • /profile now shows a quota progress bar and how much more time you need for your next promotion, and respects a grace period so a mid-week promotion doesn't retroactively flag you for missing a quota you only just inherited.
  • Shift fixes: a clear "you're already on duty" message instead of silently doing nothing, shifts left open 16+ hours from a crash now auto-close instead of getting stuck forever, an optional note you can leave when ending a shift, and paginated shift history in /duty time.
  • Application fixes: a review-and-confirm screen before final submission, a heads-up when you're running low on time to answer a question, and an Apply Again button if you accidentally decline the Privacy Policy step (no cooldown applies).
  • /ask is now capped at one request every 30 seconds bot-wide to keep AI costs in check, and the application flow skips its own Privacy Policy step if you've already agreed to the bot's Terms/Privacy Policy elsewhere.
  • Refreshed embed styling using the server's own custom emojis across Applications, /profile, and /dashboard.
