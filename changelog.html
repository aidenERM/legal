Subdivision Integration
One Clock-In, Two Guilds New Starting a shift in a subdivision server (e.g. SEU) automatically mirrors it to CHP as a linked shift. Officers never need to run two commands.
Mirror Confirmation The shift embed shows a confirmation line when the CHP mirror succeeds, or a warning if the officer hasn't joined the CHP server yet.
Auto Linked Role Auto The CHP linked role is assigned the moment a member gets their division role — or on their first shift. An hourly sweep catches anyone who slipped through.
Subdivision Roster >subdivision roster — live on-duty list with callsign sorting. "Show Full Roster" expands to all division-role holders.
Stats & Leaderboard >subdivision stats, >subdivision lb, and >subdivision compare give management a full picture of cross-subdivision activity.
Audit & Broadcast >subdivision audit flags role mismatches. >subdivision announce broadcasts a message to every linked subdivision at once.
Inactivity Alerts Auto A channel alert fires when a subdivision member goes 7 days without a shift.
Mid-Week Quota DMs Auto Every Wednesday, members under 50% of their subdivision quota receive a DM with exactly how much time they need.
RA Training System
RA Request New /ra request — Probationary Officers post a session request with optional availability. Available FTOs are pinged and can claim it with one click.
RA Host — Individual & Bulk New Individual mode: one trainee joins and the session auto-closes. Bulk mode: multiple trainees can join within a 10-minute window.
Results & Auto-Promote /ra results submits Pass or Fail. A Pass auto-grants Officer role, removes Probationary Officer, assigns the CHP role, and updates the rank tag in the trainee's nickname.
Anonymous FTO Feedback /ra feedback — trainees rate their FTO 1–5 on communication, patience, and clarity. Scores are anonymous and visible on /ra leaderboard.
FTO Leaderboard /ra leaderboard — sessions hosted, pass rate %, and average feedback score, ranked by period.
Fail Limit Tracking When a trainee hits the configured fail limit, an alert is automatically posted to the review channel for management.
Cooldown Enforcement 6-hour claim cooldown between sessions. Server-wide request cooldown prevents spam. Both are management-clearable.
Session History /ra history — full paginated log of completed sessions per FTO with results and performance notes.
Live Leaderboard
Auto-Refreshing Pinned LB New >duty livelb posts and pins a leaderboard message that updates itself every 30 seconds — no one needs to run a command.
Browse View A Browse button opens an ephemeral paginated leaderboard with a subdivision filter, showing on-duty indicators and quota badges.
Subdivision Grouping in Active UX >duty active has a new "Grouped by unit" sort mode — officers split into CHP, SEU, and other sections instead of one flat mixed list.
Category Filtering >duty active now supports Whole Department, CHP Only, or per-subdivision views selectable from a dropdown.

Background Automation
ERLC Presence Detection Auto The bot monitors when you join or leave ER:LC. Prompts a shift start on join, opens a break after 5 min away, and auto-ends the shift after 60 min away.
Stale Shift Auto-Close Auto Shifts left open for 16+ hours are automatically closed to prevent ghost entries corrupting the leaderboard.
Service Stripe Badges Auto Milestones at 10, 25, 50, and 100 total shifts are tracked and celebrated in your shift-end DM.

Quota Reminder DMs Auto Automated DMs on Friday (customizable) to anyone under their weekly quota, with exactly how much time they still need.
LOA Auto-Expiry Auto LOA roles are removed automatically when the period expires, with a DM sent to the member. LOA members are excluded from quota enforcement while away.

CHP Management Launch — Recruitment, Applications, /ask & a Big QOL Pass

Update #1 · 2026-06-26

The first full release: automatic recruitment setup, a complete in-house application system with AI-assisted review, an AI assistant that knows CHP policy, a personal /settings command, an extra layer of tools for Deputies, and a long list of quality-of-life improvements across shifts and applications.

→ Read the full Welcome Guide for a complete tour of every feature.

  • Automatic Setup resolves the Hicom, Officer, Probationary Officer, Denied, and Blacklisted roles, plus the applications-log, apply, hicom-announcements, and recruitment-report channels — creating anything that's missing.
  • New application system: a DM-based pre-screening flow followed by 23 application questions, with progress automatically saved if you time out mid-application.
  • Staff can Claim, Accept, or Decline applications, run an AI Detector and Summary, check applicant history, flag for priority review, leave notes, and view a reapplication diff — all from one embed.
  • /ask — ask the bot anything about CHP (ranks, codes, policy, vehicles) and it answers using the official CHP knowledge base.
  • /profile — shows your weekly hours vs quota, shift streak, RA pass rate, and earned badges.
  • /dashboard — a management overview combining shift, RA, and recruitment activity in one place.
  • /settings — profile visibility, timezone, mobile-friendly embeds, and notification opt-outs. Deputies get a full extra tier of personal controls.
  • /myactions — a Deputy-only view of your own recent application reviews and RA results.
  • /info — dropdown reference for 10 codes, penal/vehicle codes, loadout, vehicle regulations, and FAQ.
  • /practice — a 5-question quiz pulled from codes and policy to test yourself anytime.
  • /lookup — quickly check anyone's rank, badge number, on-duty status, and join date.
  • Automatic weekly report card DMs, quota reminders, and a weekly recruitment report.
  • Passing an RA session now also grants the Officer role and swaps PO → O in the trainee's nickname.
  • /config reorganized into focused pages (Roles, Channels & Quotas, RA Training, Recruitment) with plain-language explanations.
